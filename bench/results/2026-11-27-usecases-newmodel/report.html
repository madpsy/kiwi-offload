How many web listeners?

A listener is a web listener: a browser on kweb. Listeners are added one at a time, all listening the same way (the "listening" column), until one is refused or, with the CPU guard off, until the audio degrades. "Can't start" means the first listener was already refused: that way of listening is not possible beside that host (for example a new wide DDC when the host holds the links or the FIR), though listening another way may be. The number is how many got everything they asked for, listening that way, beside that host; the colour is the limit that stopped the next one. Listeners who share a window need one DDC between them (or none, on the host's slices); own receivers each need a DDC.
